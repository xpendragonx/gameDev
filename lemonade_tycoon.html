UPGRADES

  
Click a machine to upgrade it. Click the hopper (or use the arrow keys) to turn the lemon valve up or down: every lemon you feed costs coins, and lemons that spill are wasted. Nothing tells you where the problem is: watch where the lemons pile up and where they fall off the belt. Reach 100% efficiency and hold it to win.

  
Learn each job by hand. Mastery lets you buy its machine (Lv 1), which also unlocks the next job. Higher levels make that stage faster, but the whole line only runs as fast as its slowest stage: check the Overview tab for bottlenecks.
